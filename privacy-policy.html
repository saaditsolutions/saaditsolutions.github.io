Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Saad IT Solutions respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, store, share, and dispose of information when you contact us, use our services, or connect an Amazon seller account to our application.

Information We Collect

We may collect information needed to provide our services, including:

  • Name, phone number, email address, and business contact details.
  • Business details, seller account information, reports, invoices, registration documents, and service-related records.
  • Technical information such as login activity, device/browser information, application logs, and support communications.
  • Amazon seller data that an authorized seller permits us to access through Amazon Selling Partner API.

Amazon Selling Partner API Data

When a seller authorizes our application through Amazon OAuth, we may collect and process Amazon data only within the roles and permissions approved by the seller and granted by Amazon.

Amazon data may include marketplace information needed for application features, such as listings, inventory, orders, pricing, fulfillment status, reports, and related seller operational data. If restricted roles are approved, Amazon data may also include personally identifiable information only where required to provide seller-authorized features.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide, operate, maintain, and improve our services. This may include:

  • Providing seller support, accounting software services, reconciliation, registration assistance, billing, and communication.
  • Displaying seller dashboards, reports, listings, inventory, order status, pricing information, and fulfillment-related information.
  • Responding to customer requests, troubleshooting issues, and providing application support.
  • Maintaining security, preventing misuse, monitoring system performance, and complying with legal or platform requirements.

We do not sell Amazon data or use Amazon data for advertising or unrelated business purposes.

How We Process and Store Information

Information is processed only for business and service purposes described in this policy. Amazon data is handled only by authorized systems and authorized personnel who need access to provide, maintain, or secure the application.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational controls to protect information, including access controls, strong authentication, restricted credentials, secure configuration practices, and encryption in transit. Where supported by our infrastructure providers, we use encryption at rest for stored data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information or Amazon data. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With the user's consent or at the user's direction.
  • With service providers needed to operate the application, such as hosting, database, monitoring, email, support, or infrastructure providers.
  • When required for legal compliance, platform compliance, fraud prevention, or security investigation.
  • When required to provide seller-requested support or service delivery.

Any third-party service providers used to operate our application are expected to protect data and use it only for the services they provide to us.

Data Retention and Disposal

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide our services, support authorized seller operations, comply with legal or platform requirements, resolve disputes, maintain security, and enforce agreements.

When information is no longer required, we delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it using reasonable methods. Sellers may contact us to request correction or deletion of their information where legally and technically possible.

Access Control

Access to Amazon data and other sensitive information is limited to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis. Administrative access is restricted, credentials are protected, and access may be reviewed or revoked when no longer required.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect client information and Amazon data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no internet, software, or computer system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

User Rights and Removal Requests

You may contact us to request access, correction, or removal of your information where legally possible. If you are an Amazon seller who connected your account to our application, you may revoke application authorization through Amazon Seller Central or contact us for assistance with deletion requests.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
